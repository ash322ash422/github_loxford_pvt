# Fact-Check Report for French Revolution Paragraph

- ❌ Incorrect: The French Revolution began in 1789, not 1776.
- ✅ Correct: The French Revolution lasted until 1799.
- ✅ Correct: King Louis XVI was removed from power and executed in January 1793.

- ❌ Incorrect: The Eiffel Tower was built between 1887 and 1889, celebrating the centennial anniversary of the French Revolution, not the 50th anniversary.
