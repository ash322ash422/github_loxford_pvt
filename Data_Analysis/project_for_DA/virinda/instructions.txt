# Project Instructions: Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

You are provided with the **Vrinda Store Sales dataset**. Your task is to analyze the sales data and generate meaningful insights. Follow the steps below:

---

## 1. Dataset Understanding

**Task:** Write a brief description of what each column means.

## 2. Data Cleaning

* Check for **missing values** and handle them (drop or impute as needed).
* Check for **duplicates** and remove if found.
* Correct **data types** (e.g., date columns should be in datetime format).

---

## 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Perform the following analysis using pandas, matplotlib, and seaborn:

1. **Overall performance**

   * Total sales, total orders, and total customers.

2. **Sales trends**

   * Monthly sales trend (plot sales per month).

3. **Customer analysis**

   * Gender-wise sales contribution.
   * Age group-wise sales contribution.
   * Top states contributing to sales.

4. **Order and sales analysis**

   * Which sales channel (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) is most contributing?
   * Which product categories are performing the best?
