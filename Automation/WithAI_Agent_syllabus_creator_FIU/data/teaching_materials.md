# Teaching material catalogue

## Functional dependencies
- Armstrong's axioms: reflexivity, augmentation, and transitivity.
- Attribute closure algorithm for finding F+ implications.
- Candidate keys and prime attributes.

## Normalization
- First, second, and third normal forms.
- Boyce-Codd normal form (BCNF).
- Lossless join and dependency preservation.
- Worked relation decomposition using a student-course-enrollment example.

## Recommended activity
Give students a relation with a composite key and ask them to identify partial and
transitive dependencies before decomposing it.
